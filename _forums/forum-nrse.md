## About the forum
The Nordic Research Software Engineering forum aligns with and facilitates the work of the established Nordic-RSE community, to enable and support the development of research software engineering as a profession. The goal is to support the training and community building efforts within the Nordic-Baltic region through connecting the right people from each country with the national e-infrastructure provider organisations. CodeRefinery will provide an initial collaboration platform, as the project already works on and has discussed these topics together with the Nordic-RSE. 

Further information as well as list of participants will be updated later on.
